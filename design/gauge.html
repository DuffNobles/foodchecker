Option A: dial (follows your reference)

Option B: simple scale (alternative)

Proposed rule: compare the carbs in your portion with a limit per meal (starting point 45 g, set by you and your care team). Up to 60% of the limit is Healthy, up to 100% OK, up to 150% Not recommended, above that Avoid. More than 25 g of sugars moves the result one step worse. When the estimate is uncertain the marker shows a range instead of one spot. The rating uses carbs and sugars only.
